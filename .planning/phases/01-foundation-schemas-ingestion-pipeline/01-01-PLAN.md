# Plan 01-01: Tracer Scaffold, Dark Legal Aesthetic, Radix UI Suite, & Legal Disclaimers

## Objective
Establish the project workspace foundation with Next.js 14 App Router, strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS with authoritative dark legal tokens (`#0B0F17` obsidian, `#C5A059` / `#D4AF37` legal gold, slate borders, traffic-light risk colors), zero-CLS typography (`DM Serif Display`, `DM Sans`, `JetBrains Mono`), the foundational shadcn/Radix UI suite, and universal non-dismissible legal disclaimer surfaces. This serves as the Tracer Slice proving framework buildability, styling architecture, component library setup, and statutory non-UPL compliance.

<threat_model>
### ASVS L1 Threat Assessment

#### Trust Boundaries
- **TB-01 (Client Presentation Layer)**: The user's web browser rendering HTML/CSS/JS. Legal disclaimer elements must be structurally rendered into the DOM without user-controllable bypass, conditional unmounting, or CSS suppression that could invalidate regulatory safe-harbor protections against the Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL) under Advocates Act 1961 §§ 29 & 33.

#### STRIDE Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T-01-05** | Tampering / Compliance | `components/shared/LegalDisclaimer.tsx` & `app/layout.tsx` | High | Mitigate | Render non-dismissible disclaimer banner in root layout with fixed position (`z-50`) and no close button or dismiss state. Render viewport disclaimer card above analysis viewports. Both disclaimers contain explicit statutory non-UPL statements (educational/informational only, no attorney-client relationship, consult licensed counsel). |

#### Security Controls
- **ASVS V14.2 (Input / Output Integrity and Legal Guardrails)**: Mandatory disclaimer notice is non-dismissible and persistently anchored across all user views.
</threat_model>
