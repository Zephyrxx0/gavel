# Plan 02-03: Actionable Checklist & Lawyer Prep Guide

## Objective
Implement Layers 3 and 4 of the Document Decoder dossier: the `ActionChecklist` component (3 chronological timing groups, interactive check-off states, semantic action badges) and the `LawyerPrepGuide` component (5–8 consultation question cards, 1-click clipboard copy with Sonner toast feedback, and strategic context notes). Wire clause cross-reference buttons (`Re: Clause {id}`) across both layers and expand automated tests in `tests/decoder-components.test.ts`.

<threat_model>
### ASVS L1 Threat Assessment

#### Trust Boundaries
- **TB-01 (Client Presentation Layer)**: Handling clipboard write operations and DOM cross-referencing. Ensure clipboard writes only access the specific question string without arbitrary injection, and cross-reference interactions operate safely on DOM elements.

#### STRIDE Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T-02-06** | Information Disclosure / Non-UPL | `ActionChecklist.tsx` & `LawyerPrepGuide.tsx` | High | Mitigate | Ensure checklist steps are framed as educational procedural items and lawyer questions are framed as inquiries for licensed legal counsel, never asserting legal determinations or binding conclusions. |
| **T-02-07** | Client Denial of Service / State Desync | `ActionChecklist.tsx` | Low | Mitigate | Maintain interactive check-off states in ephemeral local React state keyed by unique item ID. Do not persist to localStorage or external state stores. |
</threat_model>
