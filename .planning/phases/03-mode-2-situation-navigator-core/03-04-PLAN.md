<threat_model>
### ASVS L1 Threat Assessment

#### Trust Boundaries
- **TB-01 (Client Memory & Persistence Boundary)**: User dispute narratives, auto-saved drafts, and structured legal analysis results exist strictly in client-side memory or tab-scoped `sessionStorage`. Zero server-side persistence, logs, or unencrypted local disk writes (CORE-04).
- **TB-02 (Statutory Disclaimer Boundary)**: Legal intelligence must be rendered in conjunction with statutory non-dismissible disclaimers to prevent user reliance on AI analysis as formal legal counsel (Advocates Act 1961 §§ 29 & 33, CORE-02).

#### STRIDE Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T-03-11** | Information Disclosure / Ephemeral Data Leakage | `app/analyze/situation/page.tsx` | High | Mitigate | Enforce zero-retention posture: resetting or navigating away unmounts state and purges `sessionStorage.removeItem('gavel_situation_draft_v1')`. No cookies, IndexedDB, or server databases. |
| **T-03-12** | Spoofing / Disclaimer Absence | `app/analyze/situation/page.tsx` | High | Mitigate | Display `<LegalDisclaimerCard />` unconditionally above all intake and dossier states. It cannot be dismissed or hidden. |
| **T-03-13** | Denial of Service / State Desync on Retry | `app/analyze/situation/page.tsx` | Medium | Mitigate | Maintain immutable request parameters during retry or category override re-runs, preventing race conditions or stranded loading states. |
</threat_model>
