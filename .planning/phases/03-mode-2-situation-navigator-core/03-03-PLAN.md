# Plan 03-03: Situation Navigator Dossier Presentation Components

## Objective
Implement the 6 presentation layers of the Situation Navigator Dossier and the docked sticky navigation bar at `/analyze/situation`. Build the top-level critical deadline alert banner (SIT-07, D-10), executive summary card with verified category confirmation and "Change Domain" override trigger (SIT-02, SIT-03, SIT-06, D-07, D-14, D-16), statutory rights Radix accordion (SIT-04, D-11), 4-tier urgency roadmap with Doable Solo badges and check-offs (SIT-05, D-05, D-12), evidentiary checklist with why rationales and progress counter (SIT-06, D-06, D-12), counsel triggers card (SIT-06, D-08), and docked sticky navigation bar (D-09). Establish comprehensive component tests in `tests/situation-components.test.ts`.

<threat_model>
### ASVS L1 Threat Assessment

#### Trust Boundaries
- **TB-01 (Client DOM Presentation Layer)**: Displaying AI-generated text, statutory citations, and procedural recommendations in the DOM. Ensure all plain-English legal explanations remain educational without prescriptive legal advice.
- **TB-02 (DOM Cross-Referencing & Interactive State)**: Ephemeral check-off state for evidence and roadmap tasks must remain in volatile memory and never persist to unencrypted local storage or leak to external services.

#### STRIDE Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T-03-08** | Tampering / Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) | Dossier Components | High | Mitigate | Render all AI-generated strings (summary, rights, roadmap, documents, warnings) as standard React text nodes (`children`), never utilizing `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. |
| **T-03-09** | Information Disclosure / Non-UPL Liability | `SituationSummaryCard.tsx` & Dossier | High | Mitigate | Prominently display the non-UPL educational notice badge: `"Educational & Informational Analysis · Not Formal Legal Counsel"`. Clearly distinguish statutory legal information from formal legal representation. |
| **T-03-10** | Client Denial of Service / Memory Corruption | `NextStepsRoadmap.tsx` & `EvidenceChecklist.tsx` | Low | Mitigate | Manage interactive checkbox states using local component state keyed by item index/id. Do not persist interactive progress to cookies or external stores. |
</threat_model>
