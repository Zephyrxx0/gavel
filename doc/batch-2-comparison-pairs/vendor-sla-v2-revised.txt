This Agreement is made on February 10, 2025, between CloudMatrix Solutions LLC ("Vendor") and Client Corp ("Client").

Vendor shall provide cloud-based API integrations and custom database infrastructure as outlined in individual Statements of Work ("SOW"). Deliverables must satisfy Client's written acceptance criteria within a 14-day review period.

Each party’s aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be capped at the total fees paid or payable by Client in the preceding twelve (12) months. 5. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client from any third-party intellectual property infringement claims arising from Vendor's code. Client shall indemnify Vendor from claims arising from Client's unauthorized alterations or misuse of deliverables.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for convenience upon thirty (30) days advance written notice. In the event of early termination, Vendor shall provide full transition assistance and export all Client data within five (5) business days at no extra charge.
